#209 · PayrollEmber POS

From the time clock to payslips

  • Pick the pay period and the hours load from the time clock: regular hours and overtime, each person's rate, and tips.
  • One tap fills SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax from the government tables. You can still change any figure.
  • Save the run and print payslips.

Staff and access

  • Everyone signs in with their own PIN. You choose what each person can see and do, and every void, refund, discount and price change has a name on it.
  • Plan the week by dragging names onto days.

Questions

Should my accountant check the first payroll?
Yes. The deductions come from the government tables, and your accountant should check the first run against your own setup.
