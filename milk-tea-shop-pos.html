A POS for milk tea shops and drink kiosks

Milk tea and drink shops sell many versions of the same drink. Ember POS keeps every version right: the price, what it uses from stock, and what the person making it sees.

Every version of every drink

  • Sizes, sinkers like pearls or nata, sugar levels and ice levels. Add-ons that cost extra charge extra; preferences like 50% sugar or less ice print for the maker without changing the total.
  • Each size and add-on can use its own ingredients, so a Large uses more tea and pearls, and your stock stays true.

Fast at the counter, clear at the station

  • A cashier taps the drink, the size and the add-ons, and the order goes to the drink station screen at once. A Now Serving screen tells customers when it is ready.
  • Hold a ticket for a group that keeps ordering, or let customers order from a QR code on their phone.

Know what you make on each cup

  • Recipes are costed from what you pay your suppliers, so you see the profit on each cup and each size.
  • Count stock, log spilled or expired ingredients, and let the Shopping List say what to buy next.

Questions

Can a sugar level or ice level be free while pearls cost extra?
Yes. An add-on with a price charges for it; one without a price is a modifier that only tells the person making the drink.

Does it work on a small kiosk with weak signal?
The Android app keeps selling during a brownout or a drop in signal, and saves everything online when the connection comes back.
