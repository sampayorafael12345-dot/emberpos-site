When you take payment, four things happen by themselves

No more writing kitchen slips, counting stock by hand, or adding up sales at night. Ember POS does all of it the moment your cashier takes payment.

Real screens from the app, version v1.5.0, using our sample café.

01 · At the counter

Take the order and the payment

Every item has its own big button. Pick the size and any add-ons, then take the payment. That's it.

  • Cash, GCash, Maya, QR Ph, GrabFood and foodpanda
  • Hold an order, split the bill, refund or cancel
  • Utang: put the order on the customer's tab
Try it in the demo (2 min)
02 · In the kitchen

The kitchen sees the order right away

The order appears on the kitchen screen with the size and add-ons. Orders change colour when they wait too long, so the cook knows which one is late.

  • The cook marks one dish or the whole order as done
  • A Now Serving screen tells customers their order is ready
  • Customers can order from their table with a QR code
Try it in the demo (1 min)
03 · In the stockroom

Your stock goes down by itself

Tell the app once what goes into each item, like 18 g of coffee and 160 mL of milk. After that, every sale takes those amounts out of your stock.

  • A warning before you run out
  • Record deliveries when they arrive
  • Record spoiled or wasted items
Try it in the demo (2 min)
04 · Your profit

See your real profit every day

Ember POS takes your sales, subtracts ingredients, staff wages and expenses, and shows you what you really earned today.

  • See which items earn you the most
  • Know how much you need to sell to break even
  • A sales report your bookkeeper can use
Try it in the demo (2 min)

Questions shop owners ask

Can I use it on my laptop or computer?

Yes. Open the app in any web browser and sign in with your shop account. It is the full app: selling, the kitchen screen, your menu, stock, reports, staff and payroll. Changes show on all your devices right away.

Can I record utang like in our notebook?

Yes. Add the customer to the order and choose Utang when paying. The amount goes on their tab. You can see who owes you, how much and since when, record their payments, and set a limit for each person. The app can also write a polite reminder you can send on Messenger.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not in the App Store yet. For now, iPhone and iPad users can use the full app in Safari, including the register. Open the app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You get an Ember POS icon that opens like a normal app.

Does it work without internet?

The Android app does. You can keep selling, printing receipts and signing in, and everything is saved online when the internet comes back. The browser version needs internet to open, then it can handle short drops. If your signal is often weak, use the Android app.

What do I need to buy?

Nothing special. Any Android phone or tablet works, even an older one. You can also use an iPhone, iPad or computer through the web browser.

Is my data safe? Can my staff see everything?

Your data is stored online, kept separate from every other shop, and backed up for you. Inside your shop, you decide what each staff member can see and do. For example, you can allow only yourself to give discounts or cancel orders.

Do I have to type in my whole menu?

No. Send us what you have, like a spreadsheet, a document or a photo of your menu board, and we will add your items, prices, ingredients and stock for free. You can also import a spreadsheet yourself, or load our sample menu to try things first.

How do I start?

Download the app from Google Play, create your shop, and load the sample menu to look around. Or email your menu to atlasco.web@gmail.com and we will set it up for you for free.
