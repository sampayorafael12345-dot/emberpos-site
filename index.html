Questions

Common questions.

Can I use it on my laptop or desktop computer?

Yes, and it is the whole app, not a cut-down version. Open the web app in any browser, sign in with the same shop account, and you get everything: the register, the kitchen screen, menu and recipes, inventory, reports, staff, payroll and settings. Changes show up on every device, live.

Can I record utang the way we do in our notebook?

Yes. Attach the customer to the order and choose Utang at payment, and the amount goes on their tab instead of the drawer. Money then shows every person who owes, how much, and how long it has been sitting. You record payments as they pay, set a credit limit per person if you want one, and tap a name to copy a polite reminder message you can send on Messenger.

Do you have an iPhone app?

Not in the App Store yet, it is being prepared. In the meantime iPhone and iPad owners run the full app in Safari, including the register. Open the app, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. You get an Ember POS icon and it opens full screen with no browser bars.

Does it really work without internet?

The Android app does, fully. Ringing sales, receipts, even signing in all keep working, and it syncs itself when the connection comes back. In a browser the page has to load once with a connection; after that it rides out short drops too. For a shop with unreliable signal, the Android app is the safer choice.

What hardware do I need?

Any Android phone or tablet, even an older one, or an iPhone, iPad or computer through the browser. There is nothing special to buy; it runs on ordinary devices you may already own.

Is my data safe? Can my staff see everything?

Your shop's data lives in the cloud, isolated per shop and backed up for you. Inside the shop, you decide who sees what. Access rights control every screen, and sensitive actions like voids and discounts can be limited to people you choose.

Do I have to encode my whole menu myself?

No. Send us whatever list you already have, a spreadsheet, a document, even a photo of your menu board, and we import your items, prices, ingredients and current stock levels for you. Free. You can also import a CSV yourself from Settings, or load a sample menu in one tap to look around first.

How do I start?

Install the app from Google Play, create your shop, and load the sample menu to try it in one tap. Or send your menu to atlasco.web@gmail.com and we will set it up for you, free.
