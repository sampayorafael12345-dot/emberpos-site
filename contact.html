What we sell, and what it costs

  • EmberPOS — point-of-sale, inventory, staff and reporting software for small food businesses, delivered over the internet to Android devices, desktop computers and the browser. Nothing is shipped.
  • ₱599.00 per month, per branch, or ₱5,990.00 per year, per branch. All charges are in Philippine peso (PHP) and no other currency is ever billed.
  • The first 30 days are free, with no card and no payment details needed to start. There is also a live demo shop you can try without signing up.
  • Month to month. No contract, no lock-in, no cancellation fee, no per-staff or per-device charges.

How it is delivered: immediately and automatically. Once the payment is confirmed, the paid period goes on to your shop and the app unlocks on every device already signed in to it, usually within seconds. You can check it yourself under Settings → Billing & Plan, which shows the date your subscription renews on. Full detail in Returns & Refunds.

How we take payment

Subscriptions are paid either inside the Android app through Google Play, where Google is the seller of record, or on this website, where the payment is processed for us by a licensed Philippine payment provider. We never see or store your card number: it is entered on the payment provider's own page, not ours, and we never ask for card details by email or in chat. Anyone who does is not us.
