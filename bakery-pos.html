#204Bakeries

A POS for bakeries and pastry shops

A bakery makes things ahead and sells them through the day. Ember POS records each batch, its real cost, what sold and what was wasted.

Try the live demo Start free for 30 days

#204 · BakeriesEmber POS

Batches, at their real cost

  • Give a baked item its own recipe. When you bake a batch at the prep station, the flour, butter and eggs come out of stock and the finished pieces go in, at what they really cost.
  • Mark an item sold out with one tap so the counter stops selling it.

Waste and big orders

  • Log what did not sell or went stale, so the loss shows in pesos.
  • Make a price quote for a party or office order, and turn it into an order in one tap when the customer says yes.

Selling

Fast counter selling on Android or any browser, with GCash, Maya, card and cash, and utang for regular customers.
