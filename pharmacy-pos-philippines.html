Ember for Pharmacy: a POS for drugstores

A counter that finds a medicine by brand or generic, sells it by the piece, banig or box, and never sells an expired batch. Behind it, a stock watch that says what to order and what will expire before it sells.

Start free for 30 days Ask us a question

#213 · PharmaciesEmber for Pharmacy

The counter

  • Type a brand, a generic name or a strength, or scan the barcode. Each medicine shows its generic, strength, form, maker, stock and nearest expiry.
  • The same generic in other brands sits right beside it, cheapest first, so the customer who asks for something cheaper gets an answer in one look.
  • Sell by the piece, the banig or the box. All three come out of one stock counted in pieces.
  • A batch past its date is never sold. The earliest good batch always goes first.
  • Prescription medicines ask for the doctor and patient details, and a partly filled prescription remembers what is still owed to the patient.
  • Senior and PWD discounts, cash, GCash, Maya and card.

Stock that thinks ahead

  • Deliveries are received with their lot numbers and expiry dates.
  • Stock watch races every batch against how fast that medicine really sells. It tells you which boxes will expire unsold, and the last day your supplier still takes them back.
  • It gives the day to order each medicine and how many boxes, never more than you can sell before the date.
  • It counts the sales you missed while a medicine sat at zero, and what customers asked for that you did not have.
  • A short daily count of a few lines, so the whole shelf gets counted without closing for a stocktake.
  • An expiry page for the weekly walk along the shelves: pull out what has expired in one tap.

The books

  • A prescription book and a dangerous drug book, filled as you sell and never edited afterwards. Print them or download them as a spreadsheet.
  • Senior and PWD medicine discounts added up by maker and supplier, as an estimate for your accountant.

Your whole product list, in one go

Bring in thousands of products from a spreadsheet. Ember fills in the generic name, strength and form from the product name where it can, and gives you a sheet to check the rest.

Branches and the back office

Each branch has its own stock and its own books. The back office at emberpos.net/office shows Stock watch, expiry and the prescription book for every branch from a computer. When one branch has boxes that will expire before they sell, Stock watch says which branch sells them faster, and sends them there with their lot and date.
