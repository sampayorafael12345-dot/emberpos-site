#211 · QR ordering & online storeEmber POS

QR at the table

Print the QR code on each table. Customers see your menu on their phone and order; you approve it first, then it goes to the kitchen.

Your own store page

  • Pickup or delivery, with a tip if they like, and orders up to two weeks ahead. Customers pay online with GCash or Maya through PayMongo, straight to your account.
  • List your shop on Ember's shop finder at emberpos.net/shops so people nearby find you. It is free, and you can switch it off.

Delivery with your own riders

When a delivery leaves, Ember makes a link for your rider. The rider opens it, taps Start delivery, and your customer watches them come on a map until they tap Delivered. The rider installs nothing.

Questions

Is there a fee per online order?
Ember takes no cut of your sales. Online card and e-wallet payments carry PayMongo's own fees.

How much is it?
